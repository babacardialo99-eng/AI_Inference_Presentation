# The Rise of AI Inference Chips

A technical presentation exploring how specialized silicon
(LPUs, TPUs, inference ASICs) is reshaping the cost, speed,
and energy economics of AI deployment.

## Topics Covered
- Throughput advantages of purpose-built inference chips
- Energy efficiency and cost per token analysis
- The CUDA moat and why GPUs still dominate
- Case study: Groq's Language Processing Unit (LPU) architecture
- Future of AI inference hardware
